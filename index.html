Questions we get asked

How much does a website cost?

The Basic Website is KSh 5,000 and the Interactive Catalog is KSh 12,000, one-time. A 20% booking payment reserves your place; the balance is due before launch. Custom-domain setup is included, while registration or renewal is charged at cost. Optional paid hosting is 15% of the package price per month.

How long does it take?

Most WhatsApp setups and one-page sites are ready within about a week, once we have your photos and product details.

Do you work with interior professionals as well as shops?

Yes. We work with home décor and furniture businesses, interior designers, makers and trade suppliers. Your site can showcase products, services or project work and direct enquiries to WhatsApp.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, business details, WhatsApp number, social links, and photos or project examples. For an Interactive Catalog, also provide the item names, short descriptions and categories.

Do you work with businesses outside Nairobi?

Yes. We work with businesses across Kenya. Everything happens over WhatsApp, so you can be anywhere.

Will customers still chat with me on WhatsApp?

Yes. The website’s job is to send people to your chat, not replace it. You keep talking to customers the way you do today.
